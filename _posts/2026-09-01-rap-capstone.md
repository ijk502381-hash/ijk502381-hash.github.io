## RESTful ABAP (RAP) Capstone Design
This is a capstone design course on the ABAP RESTful Application Programming Model.
I defined my own booking table based on the SBOOK flight data model and generated 200 test records.
I'm learning how to build business objects with CDS and RAP step by step through hands-on exercises.


## Tech
* Eclipse ADT
* ABAP Dictionary: database table, data elements, structures, technical settings
* RAP and EML (MODIFY ENTITIES)
